# What changed in the 2026 frontier models? Kimi K3 against the series (snapshot, October 2026)

## Description

A dated snapshot, read from the technical reports of Kimi K3, K2 and Kimi Linear: a clip inside the optimizer for attention scores, Attention Residuals instead of a fixed residual sum, per-head Muon, a 3:1 mix of delta-attention and full latent attention over 93 layers, and a mixture of experts that uses 16 of 896 experts per token (104 billion of 2.8 trillion parameters). Each choice is tied to the earlier part that derives the problem.

Every number in the film is computed or measured by a script you can rerun, and each spoken number is asserted against it before the voice-over is generated.
