Film one made a Transformer trainable at step zero. Films two A and two B followed the gradient and the update. This film runs the model for thousands of steps, and asks what training builds, and what changes when the model gets bigger.

We make seven stops. First, how a model learns to copy a pattern: the induction circuit, layer by layer. Second, where it keeps facts and relations. Third, attention as a memory, and what the newer architectures do with it. Then scale: how to spend a budget between model size and data, and why a learning rate stops transferring when the model gets wider. Then why a network that can memorize anything ends up learning the rule. And last, the newest frontier model, read against all of it.

Each stop has the same shape: the intuition, the math with the numbers plugged in, a plot, and the code.
